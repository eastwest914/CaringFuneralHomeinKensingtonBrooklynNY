Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first when a loved one passes away?

Call us at any hour. We are open 24 hours and will explain the next steps, arrange transfer and help you begin planning.


What is the difference between burial and cremation?

Burial places the body in a cemetery, often after a funeral or graveside service. Cremation is done before a memorial or final placement. We explain both so you can choose what fits your family.


Can I plan a funeral in advance?

Yes. Pre-planning lets you record your wishes and ease the burden on your family. Call us to start a plan at your own pace.


What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all credit cards, cash and checks. We review costs with you before anything is arranged.

Funeral Services

A funeral is the first public way a family says goodbye, and we help you shape it. We arrange the visitation, the ceremony, the procession and every detail of timing, so you are not chasing logistics during a hard week.

Services can follow your faith, your family's customs or a simple non-religious format. We coordinate with clergy, cemeteries and florists, prepare your loved one with care and keep you informed from the first call until the last guest leaves.

Burial Services

If your family has chosen burial, we take care of the arrangements that surround it. That includes help selecting a casket, scheduling with the cemetery, preparing the necessary paperwork and arranging transportation to the burial site.

We can hold a service at our funeral home, at a house of worship or only at the graveside. Our staff will explain each choice and its cost clearly, so you can decide with confidence and without pressure.

Cremation Services

Cremation can be simple or it can include a full service, and we support both. Some families hold a viewing or funeral before cremation, while others prefer a direct cremation followed by a memorial at a later date.

We explain the required authorizations and waiting steps, show you urn and keepsake options and discuss scattering or placement of ashes. Everything is handled respectfully, and you always know where your loved one is in the process.

Memorial Services

A memorial service celebrates a life and does not require the body to be present. It can take place in our chapel, in a home, at a place of worship or in a venue that held meaning for the person you lost.

We help with photo displays, music, readings, programs and guest arrangements. A memorial works well when family is traveling from far away or when you need a little time before you are ready to gather.

Funeral Pre-Planning

Pre-planning means recording your funeral wishes while you are healthy and clear-minded. You choose the type of service, burial or cremation, and share personal details such as music, readings and who should be notified.

A written plan removes guesswork and arguments from an already emotional time. It also gives you a chance to review costs and ask questions at your own pace. We will keep your plan on file and update it whenever your wishes change.

Casket Rental and Graveside Service

Casket rental is a practical choice for families who want a viewing or funeral service followed by cremation. You have the traditional ceremony and a casket present, without purchasing one that will not be buried.

For families who prefer something smaller, we arrange graveside services with a short ceremony, prayers or remarks at the burial site. It is a dignified, affordable option that keeps the focus on remembrance and the people gathered.
